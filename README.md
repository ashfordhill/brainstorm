## What Happens When Open Source LLMs Advance?

The open-source LLMs of today aren't on par with today's most expensive frontier private LLMs. However, give it a couple years (maybe longer, maybe shorter, idk. someday) and they will be just as capable as the frontier premium models of today, using less resources. 

Businesses will want to utilize open-source LLMs (handwaving security things) in order to not stay tied to token subscriptions. Tokens are not easily described in a way where you can say "X tokens will get you Y amount of work output". The current premium frontier models already aren't profitable - the subscription models are priced way too low and keep increasing in costs. These models are already advanced enough to perform a large amount of high-quality work in many fields without little human oversight. 

Internally, my company is moving towards their own VS Code fork/agent management instead of using third-party tooles and subscriptions. There are many security concerns today around open-source LLMs, but I think the idea of saving money appeals to mouth-foaming hungry business types. 


## Does AI Equate To Productivity in a Company?

Jevon's paradox.

Humans, like AI are non-deterministic factors! Some humans will innovate and contribute towards efforts that could actually increase productivity in the company. Many humans will likely pick the past of least resistance which is to have AI integrate into theirworkflows in the most simple way, performing their work for them so they can use the additional time to browse the internet (or some other activity that doesn't contribute to company productivity).

Therefore, it cannot be assumed that more AI == more productivity in the company. 

For companies truly looking to scale, you would want to be very selective with hiring and hire the 'innovate/problem-solver' types who value efficiency. Give them the tools and they can truly help streamline functions enough to replace (either mostly or entirely) certain job functions. I'm not seeing a future where it creates more jobs in this type of setting, unless the improved productivity and efficiency leads to the company venturing out into new fields/goals that would require it to hire more personnel - after all there's a limit to how much impact even the most valuable employee can have in a large corporation.

This is because the bottleneck that is being resolved is 'mental energy'. You give mental energy resource back to employees (by having them able to subcontract their work), and what is the common human tendency? To work more? Harder? Not usually. If you give a vacuum to someone with only a broom, and they are able to clean their floors in 10 minutes now instead of 15, are they going to use the saved 5 minutes to clean more? Do humans enjoy work generally (work meaning work; but what is 'work' to one person is not 'work' to another person)? It's the same way that having an 8-hr workday often doesn't mean 8hrs of productivity. And wy 4 hrs of productivity for 1 employee (e.g. - someone fresh out of college) isn't the same as 4 hrs of productivity for another employee (e.g. - someone senior and invested in positive outcomes for the company).

Because AI tools enable the general population to get more involved in software, digital tools, etc., it could become a very saturated market. On the other hand, because the field of hardware (e.g. - robotics) doesn't benefit the same growth trajectory that software does, I think that could be a more worthwhile endeavor. Let everyone chase the software goldmine and do something adjacent or unrelated, IMO (or if doing software, something niche).

Government money would also be very valuable to attain since it can be long-term and not necessarily tied to product quality but rather what metrics it passes and the shoulder-rubbing of the people involved in the deal (maybe? what do I know?).

## Reverse Engineering Cheapening

Reverse engineering can be very time and expertise-intensive type of task. However, with AI agents and all the built-in knowledge they've attained, now you can reverse engineer all sorts of software. 

See: https://github.com/storytold where someone used AI tools to reverse engineer for the Adobe suite. 

Legalities aside, I think software like this could be at financial risk for the future, considering that their IP is purely digital. Anything with a purely digital IP and doesn't involve raw creativity (music, art, etc.) is at a large risk for losing value. 

This also includes paid operating systems - there is a possibility of more platform-agnostic consumer software like video games coming into the world. 

People are even automating their home hubs because they are tired of the spying and multiple APIs (smart lights, switches, speakers, clocks, other tools). Why use all that and potentially be tied to a subscription system when you can have Claude develop your own all-in-one for your home hub while you sleep?

Mainly I have apprehension around the long-term viability of digital products. 

Also, Amazon reduced its weekly digital publishing limit from 10 items to 2 items. People are going to spam the hell out of digital content consumer platforms (music, videos, digital products on Amazon, Etsy, print-to-order, etc.). Which means high competition, which I personally like to avoid since it decreases success chances.

### @factcheck

Introducing the `@factcheck` command, exclusively for iMessage! Skip that Google search and have our built-in agent quickly factcheck recent information in your conversation, sources included.

### FAQ

> Is factchecker always right?

- Yes.

> What about my privacy?

- Digital privacy is an illusion, give up.

> I don't want this feature in my phone. Can I opt out?

- This feature will be included in all iMessage chats, as a security measure per our terms of service. If wanting to opt out, users can go to `Settings -> General -> Chats -> Privacy -> Messaging -> Privacy -> iMessage -> Force SMS` and set `Force SMS` to on. 

- This setting will be on by default. Defaults are restored every phone reboot.

`@emojifusion` is an energy-intensive command to do what users have always dreamed of: Fusing 2 emojis into 1 emoji. These are freshly generated with each command. Like a snowflake, each fusion will be unique!

#### @flip

<p align="center">
<img src=".docs/imessage-flip.png" alt="iMessage emojifusion" width="600" />
</p>


`@flip` is for users who love a little lighthearted fun in their chats. This command will flip the chat messages for all users in the current chat to become upside down! **Note that the typing box will still remain right-side up.**

 `"I'll just reverse it by sending another @flip!"` you say? Not so fast there, cowboy! `@flip` has a 10 minute cooldown. Fun for the whole family, forever!
